Plastic to Petrol Conversion through Pyrolysis

Learn how plastic waste can be turned into petrol-like substances through pyrolysis.

What is Pyrolysis?

Pyrolysis is a thermochemical process that decomposes organic materials like plastic in the absence of oxygen. The heat causes plastic waste to break down into hydrocarbons, which can be converted into liquid fuel.

The process works by heating plastic waste at temperatures between 300°C to 800°C in a reactor, which results in a mixture of gases, liquids, and solid residues.

The liquid produced is a bio-oil that can be further refined into petrol, diesel, and other fuels. The process yields around 75% petrol-like substances from plastic waste.
